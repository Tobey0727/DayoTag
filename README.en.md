## A wearable that understands you more, the longer you wear it

**Give the moments that truly mattered today back to yourself.**

DayoTag is a 24H AI memory wearable made personal, paired with a memory agent. It turns the sounds and important moments that naturally happen during your day into personal memories you can revisit, question, and act on — and the longer you wear it, the better it knows you.

**Get started:** [Download the Android APK](https://github.com/Tobey0727/DayoTag/releases) · [User Guide](docs/USER_GUIDE.md) · [Hardware files](https://oshwhub.com/eda_kqtkoxdx/project_bpeoomns) · [Report an issue](https://github.com/Tobey0727/DayoTag/issues)

## Your memories, under your control

DayoTag records the most real moments of your everyday life.

- **Real-time device–phone sync:** recording continues even when your phone is away; once reconnected, content syncs straight to your own phone over Bluetooth.
- **On-device storage, privacy in your hands:** DayoTag is a fully on-device app — all data stays local and there is no cloud service; speech-to-text can run with the on-device ASR, or you can configure a remote ASR service for better accuracy.
- **Choose your agent's brain:** the memory agent works the same way as mainstream agents such as openclaw — you decide which model provider's API to trust.

## Daily personal styling

DayoTag's low-power color display is more than a status screen — it is your personal expression surface. Favorite wallpapers, today's persona, lucky colors and AI-generated artwork can all be synced from the app to the device, so the same DayoTag changes freely with your mood, your outfit and your day.

## From recording a day to truly remembering it

1. **Hear:** the device records on-site with minimal friction; important moments can be marked with a single press.
2. **Keep:** content is stored on DayoTag first, then synced incrementally when your phone is nearby again.
3. **Distill:** the app organizes scattered audio into Today cards and browsable Memories.
4. **Resonate:** ask your own memory directly when you can't recall something, or let Skills turn results into to-dos and follow-up actions.
5. **Grow:** short-term memories settle into long-term memory, letting the agent gradually understand your interests, preferences and changes — always within boundaries you control.

## Getting started

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/readme/dayotag-quick-start.png" width="100%" alt="DayoTag setup flow: install the app, pair the device, generate memories and sync wallpapers">
</p>

For first-time setup, follow the flow above: install the app, pair the device, choose how audio is processed, and sync a wallpaper. Requires Android 7.0 or later with Bluetooth enabled; using a cloud model additionally requires network access and your own model API key.

Before installing, download the latest APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Tobey0727/DayoTag/releases) and verify its SHA-256. See the [User Guide](docs/USER_GUIDE.md) for full permission notes, step-by-step instructions and FAQs, and the [Firmware Serial-Flashing Guide](docs/FIRMWARE_FLASHING.md) for first-time flashing or recovery.
